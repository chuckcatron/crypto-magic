      How to read this page

      Short answers to the usual questions.

      Is any of this real money?

      No. This engine trades paper money only. It has no exchange account, no API key and no way to place an order. Each strategy-and-coin pair starts with its own pretend balance.

      Why run strategies that failed testing?

      All four lost money in the backtest (EXPERIMENT-008). Running them on live prices checks that the machinery works end to end, and that live results look like the test’s. Good paper results can’t make a failed strategy live: only a new pre-registered experiment can.

      What do long and short mean?

      Long bets that the price goes up. Short bets that it goes down: with futures, a fall can be a profit.

      What are the stop and the target?

      Every trade starts with two exits already set: a stop-loss that caps the loss, and a profit target. Whichever the price reaches first closes the trade. Each strategy also has a time limit.

      How much can one trade lose?

      About 0.5% of its sub-account if the stop is hit, plus costs. A sub-account that loses 2% in one day stops opening trades until 00:00 UTC.

      When does weekly momentum trade?

      Once a week, at Monday 00:00 UTC, when it re-ranks the 23 coins. Between Mondays it only closes a coin that falls 20% against it.

      How do I pause new trades?

      In the crypto-magic folder, create the kill-switch file with touch data/FUTURES_KILL_SWITCH. Open positions still close normally. Delete the file to resume.
